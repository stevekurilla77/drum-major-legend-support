Privacy Policy

Turbo Tempo Racing · a Backline Vintage game

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: Turbo Tempo Racing does not collect any data. That means no personal information, no usage data, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software in it. It does not ask for your email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission.

Your racer name and saved progress

The game can ask for a racer name (up to 12 characters) to show on the results screen. You can use any name, or none. That name and your progress (unlocked cups, trophies, best times, ghost laps and settings) are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it. You can also clear your records at any time with Reset in the Trophy Room.

Internet connection

The whole game is inside the app and plays offline. It does not need, and does not use, an internet connection.

Purchases

Turbo Tempo Racing is a paid app with no in-app purchases. Apple handles payment entirely through the App Store, and we never see your payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Sharing

We have no data about you, so we do not sell, share or disclose any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
