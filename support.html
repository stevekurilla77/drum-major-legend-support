Join Rio, the bravest Drum Major around, on a musical pixel-art adventure: explore wild worlds, battle musical monsters and bring the lost instruments home so the concert can go on.

Need help? Email rosewood70@hotmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We read every message.

How to play

  • Move: the purple arrow pad at the bottom left.
  • BATON: swing your baton to fight monsters.
  • ★ TALK: talk to characters, read signs and open things.
  • Map and pause: the two buttons at the top of the screen. The World Map also lets you travel to places you have already visited.

Saving

The game saves automatically on your device. Choose Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off. To start over, choose Begin the Quest and confirm; this erases the old save.

Saves stay on the device they were made on. They are not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes its save.

No sound?

  • Turn up the volume, and check that your iPhone is not in Silent mode (the Ring/Silent switch or Action button).
  • On the title screen, tap Test to play a test sound, and make sure the music button says Music on.
  • If sound stops after a phone call or switching apps, return to the title screen and tap Test, or close and reopen the app.

Good to know

  • The game plays fully offline. No account or internet connection is needed.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Drum Major Legend is designed for iPhone in portrait.
